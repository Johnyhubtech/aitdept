About AIT

Technology is changing.
Your skills should too.

The Department of Artificial Intelligence Technology at Federal Polytechnic Bida prepares students with modern skills in AI, machine learning, data analysis, programming and intelligent systems.

The programme combines computing foundations with practical applications, helping students understand how technology can be used to solve real problems and create useful digital solutions.
